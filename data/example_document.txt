TechCorp is a software company founded in 2010 by John Smith and Sarah Johnson.
The company is headquartered in San Francisco, California.

John Smith serves as the CEO and focuses on product strategy and business development.
Sarah Johnson is the CTO and leads the engineering team of over 200 developers.

TechCorp develops AI-powered analytics software used by Fortune 500 companies.
Their flagship product, DataInsight, processes millions of data points daily and provides 
real-time business intelligence dashboards.

The company has several key products:
- DataInsight: Real-time analytics platform
- CloudSync: Data integration service
- PredictAI: Machine learning prediction engine

In 2022, TechCorp acquired StartupAI, a machine learning startup based in Boston, Massachusetts.
This acquisition brought 50 new engineers to the team and strengthened TechCorp's AI capabilities.
StartupAI was founded by Dr. Emily Chen, who now serves as TechCorp's Chief AI Officer.

The company raised $100 million in Series C funding led by Venture Capital Partners in 2021.
Other investors include Tech Ventures Fund and Innovation Capital.

TechCorp has offices in multiple cities:
- Headquarters: San Francisco, California
- Engineering Center: Seattle, Washington
- Sales Office: New York, New York
- Research Lab: Boston, Massachusetts

The company employs over 500 people across all locations and continues to grow rapidly.

Key partnerships:
- Amazon Web Services: Cloud infrastructure partner
- Microsoft Azure: Alternative cloud platform
- Salesforce: CRM integration partner

TechCorp's revenue reached $50 million in 2022, representing 100% growth year-over-year.
The company plans to expand internationally in 2024, with offices planned in London and Singapore.
